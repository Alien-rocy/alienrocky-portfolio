About

I’m a Software Engineer with a problem-solving mindset.

I started coding at 16 and have kept going since. What hooked me was the moment a problem on paper turns into something people can actually use.

Most of what I know comes from building: practical web applications, projects with teammates, and products like StudentPal, Camply and ServeFlow. I’m drawn to the backend, to APIs, data models, and the logic that has to keep working once real people depend on it.

I’m still strengthening my foundation, especially software architecture and relational database design. I’d rather understand why something works than copy something that does. The goal stays the same: take an idea, find the real problem inside it, and turn it into useful software.
